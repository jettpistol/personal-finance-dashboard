What changed in each version

0.7.0 · Oct 27, 2026

Email notifications

  • Alerts (budgets, items to confirm, the monthly .docx review) can now arrive by email. Free with a Gmail app password.
  • Ajustes → Notificaciones: type your address, save, and use Enviar prueba. The card shows whether sending is set up.
  • Telegram is now optional and shown last.

Exchange rate in conversions

  • A transfer between a peso and a dollar account has a Tipo de cambio box (pesos per dollar, with today's BNA quote as a reference). Type the rate or the amount received and the other is calculated.
  • The Movimientos list shows what came in and the rate, e.g. "→ US$ 120,00 · TC $ 1.250".

Suggested rules

  • Reglas sugeridas in Reglas: about 120 ready-made rules for Buenos Aires (supermarkets, delivery, transport, services, subscriptions, health, shops, outings, travel, taxes and fees, income), with categories and tags.
  • Browse by section or search, tick what you use, and add it. Rules you already have show "Ya está". Nothing is applied until you add it.
  • "Aplicar reglas a movimientos sin categoría" is now just Aplicar reglas (the explanation is in its tooltip).

0.6.0 · Oct 27, 2026

New: Deudores

  • A section to note money and things you lend: who, how much (or what), when, and by when it should come back.
  • Partial returns with a progress bar; the loan closes itself when it's fully paid. You can also forgive the rest or reopen it.
  • Totals of what you're owed (pesos and dollars), things lent, and overdue loans in red.
  • Recordar copies a friendly message listing what that person still has, ready for WhatsApp.
  • Standalone for now: not linked to accounts or reports. Each of you only sees your own list.

General

  • Switch account: from the user menu, Agregar cuenta logs your partner in too, and one click switches between you without a password. Cerrar sesión only logs out the account in use.
  • Pop-up messages have the close button on the left and the colored bar on the right.
  • Dropdowns with groups (Cuenta, Categoría): group titles span the full width and open or close their entries. Only the group of the selected entry starts open; typing searches all of them.

Movimientos

  • Importar CSV in Nuevo movimiento: load many movements from one file, with a downloadable example, a row-by-row check and repeated rows skipped.
  • Cobro en cuotas: income in installments. Each cuota stays pending (money to collect) until you confirm it was paid; due ones appear in the yellow alert.
  • "Transferencia" is now Transf. propia, to tell it apart from paying someone.
  • The board filter is called Tablero: Hogar / Tablero: Iván / Tablero: Milagros, and hides the Compartido / name tag while it's on.

Cuentas

  • A list on the left (Todas, Compartido, Iván, Milagros) picks which accounts show; cards are grouped by owner on the right.

Other

  • Perfil de datos: no more "Por defecto" tag.
  • Test data: shared income (garage rent, health insurance refund), interest, sales, a sale in 3 cuotas, and sample loans in Deudores.

0.5.0 · Oct 26, 2026

Presupuestos

  • Ajustar por inflación is now a checkbox instead of a button. Ticked, peso budgets are shown adjusted by the inflation published since you set them; unticked, as you entered them. Ticking it again never adds inflation twice.
  • The same checkbox replaces the automatic option in Ajustes → Hogar, which also compounded.
  • Each budget card says how much inflation it includes, e.g. "incluye +7,4% de inflación".
  • Budgets turn red above 95% (before: at 100%), here and on Inicio.
  • Removed the "por día" amounts and the "Pasó el …% del mes" line.
  • New Disponible card: what's left of all budgets in large type (Excedido in red when over), and below it what's left per category.

Movimientos

  • Every movement has a tag saying whose it is: Compartido, Iván or Milagros. Shared movements and anything in a joint account are Compartido.
  • The Hogar / Mío / Milagros tabs now filter the list by that tag: Hogar shows the shared ones, each person's tab shows their own. Turn off Solo del tablero seleccionado to see everything.
  • For shared income, the tooltip says who received it instead of "Pagó".

Reportes

  • "IPC últimos 12 meses" is now Inflación interanual.

Perfil de datos

  • Changing profile keeps you on the page you're on and reloads its data, from both the top bar and the Perfil de datos page.
  • The "En uso" column header is gone; the column stays.

0.3.0 · Oct 24, 2026

General

  • The title strip is the same height in every section (44px, with 30px controls), so nothing moves when you switch sections.
  • Switches have the familiar rounded shape; the text next to them no longer toggles them, only the lever does.
  • Version number in the footer.

Data profiles

  • New Perfil de datos section in the sidebar, and a button with the current profile at the top right.
  • Unlimited profiles, each with its own data and the same users. A new one starts empty.
  • With no previous choice the app opens on Vacío (empty); after that it remembers the last one picked. The test data is in Prueba (demo).

Forms

  • On an error, the fields at fault turn red (Nuevo movimiento, Nuevo recurrente, Reglas, Cambiar contraseña).
  • In the category dropdown, group names show as blue headings.
  • New Ingreso compartido (shared income), for movements and recurring items.
  • Split editor: names stacked one below the other with small boxes. The default option is called Cincuenta-cincuenta; Partes iguales only appears when the default split isn't equal.

Nuevo recurrente

  • The estimated amount is large and at the top, in a terminal-style font: red with − for expenses, green with + for income. It stays when you switch type.
  • ARS / USD next to the amount opens a menu; the account list then shows only accounts in that currency.
  • For income: income examples in Descripción, Próximo cobro instead of Próximo vencimiento, and Quién paga instead of Comercio.
  • Frecuencia without the up/down arrows.

Recurrentes

  • Para confirmar is no longer a permanent section: a yellow alert with Revisar appears only when something is due.
  • No section titles: the table is the page.
  • Pausing or resuming a recurring item no longer shifts the columns.
