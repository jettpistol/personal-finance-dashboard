# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A couple sharing a household in Argentina (no car, no children, simple spending). Both partners have their own login and both enter transactions. They use it mainly on a PC (desktop browser); the phone is a secondary, mostly view-only screen.

## Product Purpose

Finanzas is a free, self-hosted personal finance dashboard for the two of them. It tracks joint and personal money in ARS and USD, splits shared expenses Splitwise-style, keeps budgets and savings goals, and makes the monthly review together easy. Success: logging stays a seconds-long habit, imports from each bank are painless, and the monthly sit-down gives them a clear, calm picture they can act on.

## Positioning

Built for one specific household in Argentina: every amount can switch between pesos and dollars at the BNA quote of its own day, inflation (INDEC CPI) is a first-class lens on spending and budgets, and the model separates the joint pot from each partner's own money (with privacy) instead of treating the household as one account.

## Operating Context

- Quick entries on PC, a few times a week, right after spending happens.
- Batch imports of bank and card statements (CSV/XLSX) weekly or monthly, using per-bank presets and categorization rules.
- A monthly sit-down together on one screen ("Cierre de mes"), with a .docx summary sent by Telegram.
- Phone check-ins are not a primary use.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Scopes: *Hogar* (joint accounts + shared expenses), *Mío* (own accounts + own share), and the partner's dashboard when shared.
- Accounts: bank, cash, credit card, savings, investment, digital wallet; ARS or USD. Transfers between own accounts never count as spending; transfers into the joint account count as contributions.
- Shared expenses with default/equal/percent/exact splits, balances and settle-up; installments (cuotas) as committed future spending; recurring bills that must be confirmed; rules; CSV/XLSX import presets; budgets with alerts and CPI indexing; goals; reports (trend, yearly, savings, net worth, per person, merchants, inflation); monthly review with notes and .docx; receipts, notes and tags; CSV/Excel export; backups.
- Privacy: private accounts and private transactions are hidden from the partner everywhere.
- Stack: React + Mantine 8 + @mantine/charts (Recharts) on Vite; Fastify + SQLite backend. Self-hosted in one Docker container.
- UI language: Spanish (Argentina, voseo). Amounts formatted es-AR (`$ 1.234,56` for pesos, `USD 1.234,56` for dollars; never `US$`).
- All existing features, routes and data must keep working in a redesign; the user allowed restructuring navigation and layouts.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Finanzas".

## Evidence on Hand

- Demo data generated by `npm run demo` (users ivan / milagros; synthetic exchange rates and CPI, labelled as demo). No real user data, testimonials or claims exist and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Calm clarity over density: the monthly picture must be readable together without wading through walls of numbers.
2. Serious, not playful: no gamification, cartoon tone or emoji-driven UI.
3. Entering and importing must stay fast; the most frequent task gets the shortest path.
4. Both currencies and inflation are always one switch away, never a separate tool.
5. Joint vs personal money, and what is private, must be obvious at a glance.
